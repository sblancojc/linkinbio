Meet Susan

About Susan Blanco

I know what it feels like to move your whole family somewhere new. I've done it more than once.

I grew up in Sydney and Brisbane, Australia. I came to Utah for university and ended up staying for more than 20 years.

Then life took us to Houston, and after that, nine years in Dubai. We loved it. But as our kids got older, we knew what we wanted for them. Real seasons. A yard. Mountains close by. Neighbors who know your name.

So in 2020, we moved our family back to Utah.

The spreadsheets. The what ifs. Leaving people you love behind. I've lived all of it. That's why I do this work, and why I help families relocating from California, Arizona, and overseas find the right home in Lehi, Draper, and the Wasatch Front.
