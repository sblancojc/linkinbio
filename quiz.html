Questions People Ask Me

What's the best Utah neighborhood for a family relocating from California or Arizona?

It depends on what you're optimizing for. For mountain and ski access, look at Cottonwood Heights, Draper, and Millcreek. For a tech-corridor commute with a mix of newer and established neighborhoods, Lehi, Saratoga Springs, and American Fork are popular choices. For a walkable, local feel, look at Sugar House and Salt Lake City. Most relocation clients compare two or three of these side by side.

Lehi or Draper, what's the real difference?

Both sit near the point of the mountain where Salt Lake and Utah counties meet. Lehi is in Utah County at the heart of the Silicon Slopes, with big shopping areas and lots of newer neighborhoods. Draper is in Salt Lake County, with the Corner Canyon trail system and quick access to the Cottonwood Canyon ski resorts. The better fit usually comes down to your commute and how you spend your weekends.

Where can I live close to skiing?

Cottonwood Heights sits at the base of Big and Little Cottonwood Canyons, and Draper and Millcreek are close too. Snowbird, Alta, Brighton, and Solitude are roughly 20 to 30 minutes from these areas, depending on the day and the canyon.

What are the most walkable areas on your list?

Sugar House and Salt Lake City (including the Avenues and Capitol Hill) are the most walkable, with local coffee shops, restaurants, and farmers markets. Sugar House also has TRAX light rail access. Millcreek adds a neighborhood feel with local shops along 3300 South and 3900 South.

Which cities have the lowest median prices?

Using the figures in my relocation guide, Provo, Eagle Mountain, and Orem have the lowest median prices on this list, while Draper and Cottonwood Heights have the highest. Prices change often, so I'll send you current numbers for the areas you're considering.

Sugar House, Millcreek, and Cottonwood Heights are the closest, around 10 to 25 minutes, while Lehi is about 30 minutes and Saratoga Springs and Provo are longer. Rush-hour traffic changes this, so test the drive at the time you'd actually commute.

Do I need to visit Utah before buying?

I recommend it. Most of my relocation buyers plan a 3 to 4 day visit so we can tour homes, explore neighborhoods, and drive the canyons before committing. Many start by taking this quiz, then narrow to two or three areas for the visit.
